# Anukili: treatment

Fiction based on the Igbo myth of Anukili Ugama, set in a mythic past.

## The world

Aguleri, in the time before anyone wrote anything down. Water comes from the local stream, and the women of Eziagulu carry it home in pots. Across the village runs the Omabala River.

## Characters

- **Anukili Ugama:** huge and tall, with tough, coarse skin, a stomach like a big water pot, a scarred face, bloodshot eyes, lips as thick as tree bark and teeth like elephant tusks. He carries a tree stump, a bow and arrows, and a big stick with thorns at the head.
- **Enyinyi:** Anukili's confidant and medicine man.
- **Anukili's father:** rejects him at birth.
- **The people of Eziagulu:** his own people, in Aguleri.

## Part 1: The Omen

A boy is born in Aguleri legs first, with upper and lower teeth, clutching roots and herbs in both hands. His father calls it an abomination, and in his rage decides to kill the baby on the fourth day. The child lives. He cannot cry until he is seven years old, and then only three times a day: at midnight, in the morning and in the evening. When he cries, animals take to their heels.

## Part 2: The Weight

Anukili becomes a leader with no training at all. He eats the food meant for thirty men. Anything he touches comes crashing to the ground. Enyinyi, his confidant and medicine man, stays at his side.

## Part 3: The Stream

A hippopotamus starts attacking people at the stream. Anukili asks if they're sure it's a hippopotamus, and calls it tiny. He tells them to show him where it waits, then he kills it. Aguleri has its hero.

## Part 4: The Pots

At thirty, Anukili turns on his own people in Eziagulu. He boasts that no man can kill him, and takes for himself the powers of the supreme being and the ancestors. No quantity of water satisfies him, so he snatches the women's water pots and breaks them with one finger. He threatens and beats people, and many die. One night they run across the Omabala to escape him. He finds them, and they run back across the river to endure him again.

## Part 5: The Fire

Owl's droppings blind Anukili. Enyinyi's herbs restore his sight, and Anukili shouts that Enyinyi is a man of God, the first time he has ever spoken of God. But the people of Eziagulu have vowed to kill him. Their first plan fails. Their next puts burning fire on his head, and he dies. The people are in ecstasy. They can't believe their eyes. So Anukili could die after all?

## The channels

Each channel holds something the others don't. These three secrets are additions for the transmedia demo and aren't part of the myth. Change or remove them as you like.

| Channel | What it covers | What only it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Animated series, five episodes | Anukili's life, as above | Why the baby lived past the fourth day |
| Game | The hunt for the hippopotamus at the stream | Something the stream hides |
| Shorts | The women of Eziagulu, each telling her own story | Who first spoke the vow to kill him |

## Who knows what

- Why the baby lived: only the series
- What the stream hides: only the game
- Who first spoke the vow: only the shorts
- Who the fire plan came from: nobody yet
